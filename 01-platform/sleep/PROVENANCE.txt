hp-firmware-idle.patch: opt-in exact-DMI firmware-coordinated LPI and
tick-freezing s2idle; Ryan Hughes' DRIPS latency adjustment with saturation.
companion/: GPL-2.0-only exported-API implementation of NVIDIA LPS0 protocol
(NVIDIA/NV-Kernels PR656 / Kaushal Rajeev Butala), with PR1958 late/early
EC settling and Ryan Hughes' PCI-PM L1.2 mask. Original path was missing LPS0.
Omarchy references b1b4608a90eb3c4e0cd12905434def2798f6e85a
and PR1958 e84963c2d9ffdeaa730e7cddc41e429802c01564.

1096-eint-wake.patch: unchanged NVIDIA patch by Kaushal Rajeev Butala,
0f0aeba492533b9dba991f131289d4a17c1f3c5d, from the above Omarchy tree.
Its authorship, sign-off and patch bytes are retained. GPL-2.0-only.
The HP kernel diff and allocator patch also modify GPL kernel source.

Trial command line additionally needs:
processor.hp_n1x_firmware_idle=1 hp_spark_wake_trial=1
nvme.quirks=1e81:6d14:simple_suspend
Exact drive: Ramaxel RPEYJ1TBMNV1QWX / HPS1, PCI 1e81:6d14.
Do not apply the quirk indiscriminately to replacement drives.
Do not combine this companion with an in-kernel LPS0 implementation.

Physical validation: one HP, short s2idle tests. Latest trial consumed
0.047Wh over 142.996s (137.779s suspended): 1.183W whole interval.
Wake was observed; kernel wake IRQ was internal touchpad. Lid-only wake
is not confirmed. Long/overnight/repeated-cycle/Omarchy validation pending.
deep mode immediately resumed and is not a qualified sleep path.
See docs/sleep.md for warnings observed during resume and measurement limits.

No AML tables, proprietary tuning blobs, kernel/module binaries or private
signing keys are distributed here. Working directories contain local build
outputs; do not publish them as release assets without a separate license,
